## Informed Consent Details

- **Specific Purpose:** Assessing the functional, psychological, and medical impact associated with weight and body mass using the Edmonton Obesity Staging System (EOSS).
- **Data Collected:** Physical health stage (`eoss_physical_health_stage`), mental health stage (`eoss_mental_health_stage`), and functional impairment stage (`eoss_functional_stage`).
- **Calculated Scores / Indicators:** EOSS Overall Staging (Stage 0 to 4), indicating clinical risk severity.
- **Visibility:** EOSS stage and functional impact ratings are accessible to authorized administrators of your Organization.
- **Non-Diagnostic Notice:** EOSS classifies clinical severity to guide preventive lifestyle interventions; it does not replace individualized medical management.
- **Revocation:** You may revoke consent for this form at any time.
