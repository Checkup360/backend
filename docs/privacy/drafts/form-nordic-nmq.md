## Informed Consent Details

- **Specific Purpose:** Screening for work-related musculoskeletal symptoms, discomfort, and functional limitations across anatomical regions (Standardised Nordic Questionnaire).
- **Data Collected:** `nordic_nmq_data` (JSON mapping reported discomfort, pain, and activity prevention across neck, shoulders, elbows, wrists/hands, upper back, lower back, hips/thighs, knees, and ankles/feet over the past 12 months and 7 days).
- **Calculated Scores / Indicators:** Musculoskeletal Symptom Index and regional ergonomic risk flags.
- **Visibility:** Musculoskeletal symptom map, summary risk levels, and specific anatomical responses are accessible to authorized administrators of your Organization for ergonomic and preventive health interventions.
- **Non-Diagnostic Notice:** This questionnaire serves as an ergonomic screening tool. It does not replace orthopedic, physiatric, or radiological medical diagnostic evaluations.
- **Revocation:** You may revoke consent for this form at any time.
