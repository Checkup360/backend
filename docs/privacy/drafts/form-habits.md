## Informed Consent Details

- **Specific Purpose:** Assessing type 2 diabetes 10-year risk and evaluating lifestyle risk factors (physical activity, nutrition, smoking, family history).
- **Data Collected:** Daily physical activity (`daily_physical_activity`), daily vegetable and fruit consumption (`fruits_vegetables`), use of blood pressure medication (`hypertension_medication`), personal history of high blood glucose (`previous_high_glucose`), family history of diabetes (`family_diabetes`), and smoking status (`smoker`).
- **Calculated Scores / Indicators:** FINDRISC (Finnish Diabetes Risk Score) and cardiovascular risk contributions.
- **Visibility:** Diabetes risk score, percentage risk estimation, and individual responses will be accessible to authorized administrators of your Organization.
- **Non-Diagnostic Notice:** FINDRISC is an epidemiological risk estimation tool. It does not replace diagnostic blood tests (such as oral glucose tolerance tests or HbA1c).
- **Revocation:** Revoking consent halts further habit calculations and form submissions.
