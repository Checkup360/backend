## Informed Consent Details

- **Specific Purpose:** Screening for hazardous drinking patterns and alcohol consumption risk using the validated 3-item Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test-Consumption (AUDIT-C) tool.
- **Sensitive Data Collected:**
  - `audit_c_frequency` (Frequency of drinking alcohol beverages)
  - `audit_c_quantity` (Standard drinks consumed on a typical day when drinking)
  - `audit_c_heavy_drinking` (Frequency of consuming 6 or more standard drinks on one occasion)
- **Calculated Scores / Indicators:** AUDIT-C Total Score (0–12), risk classification (Low risk, Hazardous/Harmful drinking risk), and excessive drinking flag.
- **Visibility:** AUDIT-C score, risk level, and submitted answers are accessible to authorized administrators of your Organization within workplace health wellness programs.
- **Non-Diagnostic Notice:** AUDIT-C is a brief behavioral screening instrument. A positive score indicates risk that warrants lifestyle moderation or professional consultation, but does not constitute a clinical diagnosis of Alcohol Use Disorder.
- **Revocation:** You may revoke consent for this questionnaire at any time.
