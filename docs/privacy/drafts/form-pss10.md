## Informed Consent Details

- **Specific Purpose:** Measuring the perception of psychological stress, unpredictability, and overload in daily and work life over the past month using the Cohen Perceived Stress Scale (PSS-10).
- **Data Collected:** 10 ordinal questionnaire items (`pss10_q1` to `pss10_q10`) evaluating feelings of being upset, unable to control events, feeling nervous/stressed, coping ability, and anger.
- **Calculated Scores / Indicators:** PSS-10 Total Score (0–40) and Stress Level Category (Low, Moderate, High perceived stress).
- **Visibility:** Stress score, risk classification, and individual item responses are accessible to authorized administrators of your Organization for workplace well-being programs.
- **Non-Diagnostic Notice:** PSS-10 measures subjective stress perception. It does not constitute a clinical diagnosis of anxiety disorders, clinical depression, or burnout syndrome.
- **Revocation:** You may revoke consent for this form at any time.
