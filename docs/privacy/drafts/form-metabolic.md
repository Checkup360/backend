## Informed Consent Details

- **Specific Purpose:** Evaluating metabolic health indicators, identifying criteria for Metabolic Syndrome (ATP III), and calculating 10-year cardiovascular mortality risk (SCORE2).
- **Data Collected:** Triglycerides (`triglycerides`), total cholesterol (`total_cholesterol`), HDL cholesterol (`hdl_cholesterol`), systolic blood pressure (`systolic_blood_pressure`), diastolic blood pressure (`diastolic_blood_pressure`), fasting blood glucose (`fasting_glucose`), and glucose-lowering medication (`glucose_lowering_medication`).
- **Calculated Scores / Indicators:** Metabolic Syndrome Risk Score, SCORE2 10-year fatal/non-fatal cardiovascular risk estimation.
- **Visibility:** Metabolic syndrome criteria, SCORE2 risk classification, and exact laboratory parameters are accessible to authorized administrators of your Organization.
- **Non-Diagnostic Notice:** Laboratory calculations indicate statistical risk. They must be interpreted in conjunction with comprehensive clinical assessment by a physician.
- **Revocation:** You may revoke consent at any time in privacy settings.
