## Informed Consent Statement

By checking the acceptance box, I hereby grant my **free, express, and informed consent** in accordance with Articles 5, 6, and 7 of Argentine Personal Data Protection Act No. 25.326 for Checkup360 and my Organization to process my sensitive health data under the following conditions:

1. **Collected Initial Data:** Biological sex, height, weight, date of birth, and baseline physical indicators provided during account onboarding.
2. **Purpose:** Establishing my initial health profile, calculating foundational body metrics (e.g., Body Mass Index - BMI), and enabling access to workplace wellness assessment questionnaires.
3. **Employer Visibility:** I acknowledge that authorized administrators of my Organization will have access to my calculated wellness indicators, risk levels, and health status as part of the organizational health program.
4. **Non-Diagnostic Nature:** I understand that all indicators provided by the Platform are strictly orientative and preventive, and do not constitute a medical diagnosis or treatment.
5. **Revocability:** I understand that I may revoke this general consent at any time through the platform settings, which will suspend health data calculations.
