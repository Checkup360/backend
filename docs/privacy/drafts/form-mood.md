## Informed Consent Details

- **Specific Purpose:** Screening for the presence and severity of depressive symptoms over the prior two weeks using the Patient Health Questionnaire-9 (PHQ-9) instrument.
- **Sensitive Data Collected:**
  - `little_interest` (Little interest or pleasure in doing things)
  - `feeling_down` (Feeling down, depressed, or hopeless)
  - `sleep_trouble` (Trouble falling or staying asleep, or sleeping too much)
  - `tired_energy` (Feeling tired or having little energy)
  - `appetite` (Poor appetite or overeating)
  - `feeling_bad` (Feeling bad about yourself or that you are a failure)
  - `concentration` (Trouble concentrating on things)
  - `psychomotor` (Moving or speaking slowly, or being fidgety/restless)
  - `self_harm_thoughts` (Thoughts that you would be better off dead, or of hurting yourself)
- **Calculated Scores / Indicators:** PHQ-9 Total Severity Score (0–27), symptom severity category (Low, Mild, Moderate, Moderately Severe, Severe risk), and clinical attention flag for self-harm ideation (`attention_flags.self_harm_thoughts`).
- **Visibility:** Calculated mood score, risk level, contributing factors, and individual answers are accessible to authorized administrators of your Organization.
- **CRITICAL NOTICE — NOT A PSYCHIATRIC DIAGNOSIS:**
  - The PHQ-9 is a brief screening questionnaire. A high score indicates symptoms that warrant professional attention but DOES NOT constitute a clinical diagnosis of Major Depressive Disorder or any psychiatric illness.
  - **Crisis Support:** If you are experiencing distress or thoughts of hurting yourself, please reach out to emergency resources:
    - **Argentina National Suicide Assistance Helpline:** 135 (Greater Buenos Aires) / (011) 5275-1135 (Nationwide) / 0800-345-1435.
    - **Medical Emergencies:** 107.
- **Revocation:** You may revoke consent for this form at any time, immediately suspending further mood assessments.
