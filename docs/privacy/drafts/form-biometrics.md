## Informed Consent Details

- **Specific Purpose:** Evaluating fundamental body composition metrics, cardiovascular anthropometric indicators, and neck circumference parameters.
- **Data Collected:** Biological sex (`biological_sex`), height (`height`), weight (`weight`), waist circumference (`waist_circumference`), neck circumference (`neck_circumference`).
- **Calculated Scores / Indicators:** Body Mass Index (BMI), baseline risk factors for Stop-BANG, FINDRISC, and Metabolic Syndrome.
- **Visibility:** Calculated risk levels, indicators, and exact submitted biometric measurements will be accessible to authorized administrators of your Organization.
- **Non-Diagnostic Notice:** Anthropometric calculations are screening metrics for preventive health and do not substitute direct medical evaluation.
- **Revocation:** You may revoke consent for this form at any time, which blocks future updates and calculations for this module.
