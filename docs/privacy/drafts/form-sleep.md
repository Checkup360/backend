## Informed Consent Details

- **Specific Purpose:** Screening for risk of Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA) and evaluating sleep-related fatigue.
- **Data Collected:** Habitual loud snoring (`snoring`), daytime tiredness/fatigue (`tired`), observed breathing pauses during sleep (`observed_apnea`), and neck circumference (`neck_circumference`).
- **Calculated Scores / Indicators:** Stop-BANG Sleep Apnea Risk Score (Low, Intermediate, High risk).
- **Visibility:** Calculated sleep apnea risk tier, explanation, contributing factors, and individual answers are accessible to authorized administrators of your Organization.
- **Non-Diagnostic Notice:** This assessment is a validated screening questionnaire (Stop-BANG). It does not replace a clinical polysomnography (sleep study) or medical diagnosis by a sleep specialist.
- **Revocation:** You may revoke consent for this form at any time in your privacy settings.
